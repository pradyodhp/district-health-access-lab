# Research backlog

`GET /api/research-backlog` produces machine-readable questions linked to each nonpassing readiness gate, with why/current/missing evidence, expected impact, urgency, owner, status and candidate source classes. Owner is null (unassigned), status MISSING; the app does not pretend to manage assignments or source collection.

First work: verify exact pilot NFHS rows against official PDFs; reconcile geography/cohort/period; obtain distinct-person screening counts and compatible adult denominators; collect cost/effect/capacity evidence; independently review model applicability and stability. All six districts need coverage rather than borrowing one district's metadata.

Source candidates are directions for research, not verified available datasets. Do not fill gaps with national averages or generated data. The separate scenario research screen is a Research Priority Heuristic based on synthetic sensitivity, range and effort. It is not formal VOI or a ranking of real district need.
